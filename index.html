What changes between the four systems?

Ratios are sampling probabilities. Missing or unsuitable transcriptions fall back to eligible variants or Base, so actual ratios may vary.

Function-Selective Transcription (FST)

HTDemucs separates each instrumental into stems. FST transcribes four stem mixtures, sampled equally within its augmentation branch:

Stem mixtureIncluded stemsWithin branchAll FST draws
FullDrums, bass, other, guitar, piano0.250.55
HarmonicBass, other, guitar, piano0.250.15
No-bassDrums, other, guitar, piano0.250.15
Piano + bassPiano, bass0.250.15

FST uses the original transcription 40% of the time and a stem-mixture transcription 60%. Full therefore totals 55%; each other mixture totals 15%. No further MIDI augmentation is applied.

Register–Density Augmentation (RDA)

RDA uses the baseline transcription: 50% unchanged, 20% note deletion, and 30% octave folding.

OperationDraw ratioExact transformation
Function-aware note deletion0.20Keep each onset’s bass note. Other chord-function notes draw a 0.05–0.20 deletion rate; tension/passing-function notes draw 0.20–0.60.
Octave folding0.30Move eligible notes ±12 semitones toward the local register at a 0.10–0.50 per-onset rate, staying between bass and top voice.

Note functions are estimated from pitch intervals above the local bass, not inferred chords.

Evaluation protocol and scope

476 held-out songs × five windows; 1,000 shared song-cluster bootstrap draws (seed 42). Reference distributions stay fixed. APA uses PCA-32 fitted to 171 aligned reference windows from 33 pairs (89.8% variance, no whitening); rankings also hold at PCA-64.

Intervals condition on fixed references and are not corrected for multiple comparisons. HTDemucs energy shares depend on the separator.
