Implementation details

How the four systems choose their training transcriptions and apply augmentation, followed by evaluation results and paired comparisons. Training choices are made separately for each four-bar segment.

What changes between the four systems?

Ratios are nominal draw probabilities. If a selected transcription is missing, too sparse, or exceeds 25 simultaneous notes, the loader tries other eligible variants and ultimately falls back to the baseline, so realized ratios can differ slightly.

Function-selective transcription

We first separate each training instrumental with HTDemucs, then transcribe one of four function-focused stem mixtures. The mixtures are weighted equally within the function-selective branch:

Stem mixtureIncluded stemsWithin branchAll Var draws
FullDrums, bass, other, guitar, piano0.250.55
HarmonicBass, other, guitar, piano0.250.15
No-bassDrums, other, guitar, piano0.250.15
Piano + bassPiano, bass0.250.15

In Var, each four-bar segment draws the original full-instrumental piano transcription with probability 0.40 or the function-selective branch with total probability 0.60. Each mixture contributes 0.60 × 0.25 = 0.15 through the function-selective branch; because the normal transcription is also Full, its aggregate ratio is 0.40 + 0.15 = 0.55. The chosen MIDI is used without further style modification.

Rule-based augmentation

Rule always starts from the original baseline transcription. As reported in the paper, it uses two augmentation types. Each four-bar segment is left unchanged with probability 0.50; otherwise it independently draws one of the following:

OperationDraw ratioExact transformation
Function-aware note deletion0.20Keep each onset’s bass note. Other chord-function notes draw a 0.05–0.20 deletion rate; tension/passing-function notes draw 0.20–0.60.
Octave folding0.30Combines local inner-voice folding (0.20), where eligible notes move ±12 semitones at a per-onset rate drawn from 0.10–0.50 while remaining between the bass and top voice, and segment-level register folding (0.10), which draws −12, 0, or +12 semitones for the complete segment with equal probability.

These are the two augmentation families named in the paper. The function labels are heuristic rather than inferred chords: they are determined only by pitch interval above the local bass.

Evaluation protocol and scope

Each metric uses 476 held-out songs × five windows. The same 1,000 seed-42 song-cluster draws are shared across systems, retaining all five windows from each selected song. APA’s 33-pair aligned/misaligned reference, the full ground-truth FAD distribution, and the full ground-truth mean mel shape stay fixed. APA is computed in a 32-dimensional PCA space fitted to the aligned reference (no whitening; 89.8% of reference variance): its 171 reference windows cannot estimate a full 512-dimensional CLAP covariance. System rankings are unchanged without projection, at 64 or 100 dimensions, and with Ledoit–Wolf reference covariances.

These intervals are conditional on the frozen references and are not adjusted for testing many system pairs. HTDemucs energy shares are separator-dependent estimates.
